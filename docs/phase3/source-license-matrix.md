# Phase 3A source licence and rights matrix

Evidence date: **2026-07-16**

This is technical due diligence for planning, not legal advice or a claim of legal clearance. Rights in implementation code, model weights, training data, imagery, metadata, APIs, cached objects and derived descriptors are independent. Material claims were rechecked against the linked primary sources on the evidence date; terms that require a logged-in session are explicitly treated as unresolved. The machine-readable [source-policy-v1.json](../../config/corpus/source-policy-v1.json) carries every required field per source, including caching permission separately from embedding/index permission.

## Decision rules

- No explicit, applicable licence means no commercial permission.
- A permissive code or model-card licence does not grant rights in training images, benchmark images or API content.
- Permission to display an image does not imply permission to prefetch, cache, normalize, embed or index it.
- Share-alike imagery stays in a source-specific partition until counsel determines whether normalized images, descriptors and index databases trigger reciprocal obligations.
- Every admitted asset needs a source URL/ID, rightsholder or contributor, licence/version/URL, attribution, acquisition receipt, hashes and deletion state.
- A licence decision is point-in-time. API/service terms and per-asset rights must be snapshotted at acquisition.

Decision meanings:

- **GO / GO_WITH_ATTRIBUTION / FIRST_PARTY_ONLY:** acquisition-ready only after the manifest's asset-level gates pass.
- **WRITTEN_PERMISSION_REQUIRED / LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED:** no acquisition until the stated approval is recorded.
- **RESEARCH_ONLY:** isolated non-production benchmark use only, and only if its conditions are satisfied.
- **BLOCKED / UNKNOWN:** reject from any acquisition-ready manifest.

## Mandatory component-rights separation

The source-level decision is fail-closed. A permissive component does not promote a
blocked or review-held component, and rights never flow between these rows.

## Commercially cautious acquisition boundary

The only defensible initial street-level production path identified here is AtlasLens-controlled first-party capture. Partner imagery can join only under an executed source-specific agreement. Open metadata and permitted aerial products may support recall and stratification, but they do not replace street-level rights.

The following controls are mandatory even for a source marked GO_WITH_ATTRIBUTION:

1. Apply a per-asset allowlist. A source-level decision never admits a file whose licence, rightsholder, URL or provenance is missing.
2. Keep first-party, partner, permissive-open and share-alike content in separate raw stores, derivative trees and index shards.
3. Preserve attribution at asset level and render an aggregated report-level attribution ledger.
4. Never place Google Street View, Google Maps/Earth screenshots, Sample4Geo code/weights, CVUSA, CVACT or VIGOR imagery into acquisition-ready storage.
5. Keep research-only benchmark material outside production cloud paths, production indexes and investor-demo result sets.
6. Treat MegaLoc/PLONK artifact rights and reference-corpus rights as separate approval gates.

## Revocation and deletion requirements

Every source agreement or open-source admission must support deterministic removal:

1. Resolve a source asset ID to its raw object, normalized derivatives, thumbnails, descriptors, index shard, attribution rows, benchmark membership, backups and reports.
2. Mark the asset quarantined immediately; prevent new corpus versions from reading it.
3. Create a tombstone containing the request/evidence, effective time, reason, affected corpus versions and deletion job IDs without retaining the image.
4. Rebuild affected immutable index shards rather than mutating lineage invisibly.
5. Delete active objects promptly and expire encrypted backups under the documented schedule.
6. Re-run leakage and benchmark-integrity checks after removal.
7. Issue a deletion certificate/audit record and revise report-level attribution if necessary.

Contracts for first-party contributors, partners and commercial providers must say whether derived descriptors survive termination. If they do not say so, the safe default is to delete descriptors with the source asset.

## Unresolved material questions

- Whether CC BY-SA obligations from KartaView, Mapillary, Panoramax or OSV-5M extend to normalized files, descriptors, index databases or model outputs.
- Whether Mapillary and KartaView service/API terms permit the planned commercial bulk download, cache duration and persistent index.
- Whether the existing MegaLoc and PLONK artifacts have a complete commercial training/release lineage despite permissive code/model-card licences.
- Whether AtlasLens can establish a lawful KVKK basis, proportionate retention and reliable face/plate blur for national first-party capture.
- Whether a partner can prove chain of title and grant downstream commercial index/cloud/deletion rights.
- Whether HGM or a commercial satellite provider will grant the exact ML/index/output rights within the future approved budget.
- Whether Commons/OpenAerialMap uploaders have sufficient underlying rights for the exact candidate files; this is an asset-level, not platform-level, decision.

No acquisition, account creation, token creation, dataset/model download or cloud provisioning is authorized by this matrix.
